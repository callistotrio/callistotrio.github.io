Formed in Tallinn, Estonia, in 2025, Trio Callistó is an exciting new ensemble with a fresh approach to piano trio performance and programming. Their name, evoking Jupiter’s moon Callisto, Greek mythology, and the idea of beauty, reflects their artistic ethos. The trio brings together lesser-known and contemporary works with established classics of the repertoire, seeking to communicate their passion for music to every audience.

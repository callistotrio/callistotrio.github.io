About Us

Formed in Tallinn, Estonia, in 2025, Trio Callistó brings together Estonian violinist Merily Leotoots, British-Irish cellist Anna Ryland-Jones, and Estonian pianist Eliza Agajeva. Based between Tallinn and Vienna, the trio draws on the musicians’ varied backgrounds and a shared passion for chamber music and the piano trio repertoire.

Trio Callistó combines established works with lesser-known and contemporary repertoire, with a particular interest in creative and innovative programming. This interest has also led the ensemble towards commissioning new music: their first commission, a new work by Estonian composer Tõnis Kaumann, will receive its world premiere in Tallinn in January 2027.

The trio’s name reflects both a fascination with astronomy and a simple idea at the heart of their music-making. Callistó, one of Jupiter’s largest moons, takes its name from the figure of Greek mythology, while the name itself is derived from the Greek kallistē, meaning “most beautiful”. For the trio, the name brings together the beauty of music with a shared curiosity about the world beyond it.

Since its formation, Trio Callistó has performed across Estonia, where the ensemble was founded. In January 2027, the trio will make its first international appearance in Bratislava, marking a new stage in the ensemble’s development.

Alongside their work as Trio Callistó, all three musicians maintain active individual careers as soloists, chamber musicians, and collaborative artists.
